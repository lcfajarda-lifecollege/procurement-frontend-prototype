# Prototype Sample Records

The prototype adds four clearly labeled sample records, four fictional vendors,
and four products once per browser. Existing records are preserved; reloads do not
duplicate samples or reset their workflow progress.

| Record | Stage | Review As |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PR-DEMO-1001: Classroom technology refresh | RFQ Sent | Procurement Officer |
| PR-DEMO-1002: Faculty workshop furniture and supplies | Requester selection, Furniture and Operational supplies lots | Requester |
| PO-DEMO-1003: Staff laptop replacement | Department Head approval, with sample DT sign-off | Department Head |
| PO-DEMO-1004: Faculty room chair delivery | Received; invoice upload available, awaiting APS admin closure | Procurement Officer |

For the technology RFQ, use **Mark quotations received (Demo)**, then submit the
quotations for review. Switch to **DT Department** to complete technical review,
then **Requester** to select the quotation.

For vendor documents, open a sample vendor, choose **Request information**, open
the vendor form, and go to **Payment & compliance** after completing the required
company/address fields.

All sample names, contacts, quotations, histories, and approval details are
fictional. No files, sent emails, real signatures, or confirmed APS payments are
fabricated. Attach only test documents. Data and uploaded files remain local to
the current browser; the actual APS connection is not active.
